Nashi Fedora port

Original Nashi source and artwork: Copyright (c) 2026 Orkun Işıtmak
and Nashi contributors, MIT. The original LICENSE accompanies this package.

This port reuses Nashi.Core. Its dependency and external-tool notices are
included in UPSTREAM-NOTICES.txt; external media tools are not in this package.

Additional UI/runtime components:
- Avalonia, Avalonia.Desktop, Avalonia.Themes.Fluent, Avalonia.Headless:
  MIT, https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia/blob/master/licence.md
- SkiaSharp: MIT, https://github.com/mono/SkiaSharp/blob/main/LICENSE.md
  Skia native code: BSD-3-Clause and third-party licenses shipped upstream.
- HarfBuzzSharp: MIT wrapper; HarfBuzz uses the MIT-style Old MIT license.
- .NET runtime: MIT, https://github.com/dotnet/runtime/blob/main/LICENSE.TXT
  Runtime third-party notices accompany the self-contained distribution.
- LibVLCSharp 3.10.2: LGPL-2.1-or-later, VideoLAN and contributors.
  Source and replacement libraries: https://code.videolan.org/videolan/LibVLCSharp
  The wrapper remains a separate replaceable DLL. Native libVLC and its plugins,
  Poppler and Tesseract are installed from Fedora packages rather than bundled.

Exact transitive dependency versions are pinned in linux/Nashi.Linux/packages.lock.json
and windows/Nashi.Core/packages.linux-x64.lock.json. Package metadata and available
full license texts, native dependency notices and .NET runtime notices are included
in the licenses/ directory. This package is an independent port,
not an official upstream Nashi release.
